Replication 1 = runs/ex10_faster.log (already measured). Replications 2 and 3 = runs/ex10_faster_rep2.log, runs/ex10_faster_rep3.log, same script and settings.
For each variant V in V1, V2, V3, pooling the 9 scored runs of V and the 9 scored runs of B over the three replications:
  (1) count the replications in which best(V) < best(B);
  (2) median of V's 9 runs and median of B's 9 runs;
  (3) the share of the 81 (V run, B run) pairs in which the V run is faster.
V counts as faster than B only if: (1) = 3 of 3, AND median(V) < median(B), AND (3) >= 75% of the 81 pairs, AND V's plan differs from B's plan.
V3 has the same plan as B and serves as a noise control: if V3 also meets the three timing conditions, the timing difference of any variant cannot be attributed to its plan change, and no variant is declared faster.
The first replication's own rule and result stay as reported; nothing is removed or re-run selectively.
